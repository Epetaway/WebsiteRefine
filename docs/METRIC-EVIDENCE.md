## Evidence package required for a future metric

Every future metric must include the claim wording, employer/project, original source, measurement method, measurement date, Earl’s exact contribution, publication permission, supporting record, and a verified reviewer. A metric should also state its baseline and avoid implying causation when the evidence supports only correlation.

## Numbers that remain

Dates, document page counts, route counts, and dated QA results may appear where they are directly generated or verified. They are operational facts, not marketing outcome claims. The public copy uses “three investigations” because the site visibly contains exactly three governed investigation records.
