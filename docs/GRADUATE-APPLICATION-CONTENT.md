## Five-minute reviewer position

Earl Hickson Jr. is a UX Engineer and independent HCI practitioner whose path moves from graphic design through interaction design, frontend engineering, and full-stack product development toward rigorous study of human consequences.

Primary thesis:

> I design and build technology that helps people become more capable.

Supporting belief:

> Technology should support human capability without unnecessarily replacing human agency.

## Why HCI

Graphic design supplied preparation in hierarchy, typography, composition, and visual communication. Engineering made interaction behavior and system constraints concrete. Building full products answered whether a system could function, but not whether it helped someone understand, decide, learn, or act. Graduate study is the path to the human-research methods needed to evaluate that difference.
