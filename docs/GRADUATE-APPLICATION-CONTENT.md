## Why HCI

Graphic design taught communication. Interaction design introduced behavior.
Frontend engineering made behavior real. Full-stack development exposed the
system beneath the interface. HCI supplies the methods needed to evaluate the
human consequence instead of treating a working implementation as proof of a
human outcome.
