# WithYou — asset provenance manifest

Audit date: 2026-10-25. Every image on `/work/withyou` is listed here with where it came from and what it is allowed to claim. The per-file sizes and SHA-256 values are in `client/src/data/withyou.media.json`, which the build script writes.

## How the files were made

- **Sources.** The WithYou app repository (`TerrianAi/withyou`, private, checkout at `612da33`, Oct 13, 2026) and the WithYou design-system export (`Design/withyou`, files dated May 10, 2026). Both are read, never modified.
- **Vectors** (9 brand files, 39 icons, 8 illustrations) are copied byte for byte from `apps/mobile/assets/` and their SHA-256 recorded. The copier refuses any SVG with script, event handlers or remote references.
- **Raster derivatives** (AVIF + WebP, metadata stripped) are generated by `scripts/withyou-assets.mjs`. Crops are fixed rectangles recorded in the media JSON. Nothing is retouched, recolored or composited.
- **Design renders.** The design files are self-contained HTML exports (React + Babel). `scripts/withyou-design-captures.mjs` serves them read-only and captures them at 2× with Playwright. A few captures click the export's own platform/theme toggles first. One extra page, `scripts/withyou-harness/social-posts.html`, renders the marketing kit's `SocialPost` component — the kit defines it but its index page never shows it.
- **Native captures** are the app repository's own QA screenshots (iOS Simulator and Android emulator, dated in their folders and PRs), plus two Maestro tour screenshots from the local run of May 30, 2026 (`.maestro/screenshots/` is gitignored in the app repo).
- **Two compositions** (the card preview and the social image) were laid out for the portfolio from three real QA captures and the lockup. They contain no invented screens.
- **Diagrams and demos** drawn in HTML/CSS on the page (the mark construction, the visibility layers, the photo-treatment layers, the safe-text zones) carry a "Retrospective illustration" label and are not files.

To rebuild:

```bash
# 1. Renders of the design source files (needs Playwright's Chromium or Chrome)
DESIGN_DIR=../../Design/withyou APP_REPO_DIR=../withyou CAPTURES_DIR=tmp/withyou-captures \
CHROME_BIN=/path/to/chrome [VENDOR_DIR=/path/to/node_modules] [ONLY=id,id] \
node scripts/withyou-design-captures.mjs

# 2. Derivatives, vectors and the media record (needs sharp, which the portfolio doesn't install)
SHARP_FROM=/path/to/a/project/with/sharp/package.json \
APP_REPO_DIR=../withyou CAPTURES_DIR=tmp/withyou-captures COMPOSITIONS_DIR=/path/to/compositions \
node scripts/withyou-assets.mjs
```

`VENDOR_DIR` serves React, Babel and the two fonts from a local `node_modules` instead of unpkg and Google Fonts, for machines without network access. Re-running step 2 on unchanged sources reproduces byte-identical files.

## Status vocabulary (as shown on the page)

- **Simulator / emulator capture** — the running app, on the platform and date given.
- **Prototype reference** — the clickable v1.0 prototype, not the app.
- **Design source** — a render of a design file (style guide, workflow map, handoff, marketing kit).
- **AI-generated placeholder** — the couple photography (below).
- **Superseded** — earlier logo and icon files, shown as history.
- **Retrospective illustration / Computed for this page** — drawn or measured while preparing the page.
