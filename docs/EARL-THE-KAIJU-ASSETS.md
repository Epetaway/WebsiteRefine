# Earl the Kaiju — asset provenance manifest

Audit date: 2026-10-24. Every image on `/work/earl-the-kaiju` is listed here with where it came from and what it is allowed to claim.

## How the files were made

- **Logos** are copied byte for byte from the approved brand package and checked against its `MANIFEST.txt` SHA-256 values. Nothing is redrawn or retyped.
- **Raster derivatives** (AVIF + WebP, two or three widths, metadata stripped) are generated by `scripts/earl-the-kaiju-assets.mjs`. Sources are never modified. Proof images are fixed-rectangle crops — no retouching, recoloring or compositing.
- **Website screenshots** were captured with Playwright at 2× from a local production build (`next build` static export, served locally) of `Epetaway/EarltheKaiju` main @ `77657a8`. The site is not deployed.
- **Kaiju Coach screenshots** are CI renders (Roborazzi under Robolectric) committed in the public app repository at `6446efa`, with fictional data. One image comes from the CI emulator run.
- **Two compositions** (the card preview and the social image) were rendered for the portfolio from the approved crest/lockup and the real captures. They are labeled as reconstructed compositions here and contain no invented screens.
- **Fonts:** Barlow Condensed 600/700/800 (SIL OFL 1.1, from @fontsource via the website repo) are self-hosted in `client/public/assets/fonts/` and loaded only by the case study's stylesheet.

To rebuild:

```bash
SHARP_FROM=/path/to/a/project/with/sharp/package.json \
BRAND_PACKAGE_DIR=../../Design/EarltheKaiju/Earl-the-Kaiju-Brand-Package-v1.5 \
APP_REPO_DIR=../earltheKaiju_app \
SITE_CAPTURES_DIR=/path/to/captures COMPOSITIONS_DIR=/path/to/compositions \
node scripts/earl-the-kaiju-assets.mjs
```

Requires `pdftoppm` (poppler) for the guideline pages.

## Status vocabulary

- **Approved** — signed off by Earl in the brand package's approval record (`09-approval-record/decisions.md`).
- **Proposed** — in the package but still subject to design review (tagline settings and marketing templates).
- **Exploratory / source reference** — not production art. Only the raster concept board is in this category.
- **Implemented** — a capture of running software (local build or CI render).
- **Reconstructed** — a new layout or diagram made for the portfolio from real assets or documents. The timeline, site map, inquiry pipeline and ecosystem diagrams on the page are HTML, carry a "Retrospective illustration" label, and are not listed below as files.
