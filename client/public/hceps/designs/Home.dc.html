A portfolio in the form of a thesis.

I design and build technology that helps people become more capable.

Technology is both a creative medium and a functional system. Its value comes not only from how it looks or what it can automate, but from what it enables people to understand, decide, learn, create, and do. It should support human capability without unnecessarily replacing human agency.

FOCUS Human capability — what technology helps a person become able to do
METHOD Build to find out — working software reveals what mockups cannot

01 THROUGHLINE

A creative practitioner whose questions kept expanding.

The path runs from art through graphic design, interaction design, and frontend engineering, into full-stack engineering and now human-computer interaction. Each stage widened the question — from how something should look, to how it should work, to what it helps a person become capable of doing.

Engineering became another creative material.
