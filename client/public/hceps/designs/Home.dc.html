01 / RELATIONSHIPS WORKING BETA

How might a shared digital space support intentional connection — without replacing the conversations that matter?

WithYou — pairing is not sharing.

A private space for two people: mood check-ins, shared notes, and love-language prompts. The couple pairs once, then the app works to point them back toward each other instead of the screen.

EVIDENCE 531 INTEGRATION TESTS · 17 E2E FLOWS · EXPO + POSTGRES
FINDING Connection needs prompts, not feeds — the interface should end sessions, not extend them.

02 / LEARNING ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

How can digital tools support coaching and progression — without reducing learning to a checklist?

DojoNet — progress is not completion.

A training platform for martial-arts schools: session journals, a coach-facing feed, and roll-partner matchmaking. Built from a decade on the mat — teaching BJJ is the user research.

EVIDENCE WORKING REACT APP · JOURNAL, FEED, MATCHMAKING LIVE
FINDING Learners need a record of feel, not just reps — journals beat progress bars.

03 / DECISION SUPPORT FUNCTIONAL BETA

How can an interface help someone navigate a complex decision space — without deciding for them?

Gundam Forge — more information is not a better decision.

A deck builder and playtest simulator for the Gundam card game: live rule validation, a meta dashboard, and a builder that surfaces trade-offs — then leaves the choice to the player.

EVIDENCE 623-CARD CATALOG · 248 PASSING TESTS · NEXT.JS
FINDING Constraints beat catalogs — showing what a deck can't do teaches faster than listing what cards do.

WORKING PRINCIPLES
P·01 Capability over convenience The goal is what someone becomes able to do, not just what the tool does for them.
P·02 Agency before automation Support human decision-making; don't replace it.
P·03 Evidence before claim Don't present what is designed as what is shipped.
P·04 Build to find out Working software reveals what mockups cannot.

PRELIMINARY — These independent investigations use working software to make human-centered questions concrete. They are informed by HCI principles but have not yet undergone formal research evaluation.
