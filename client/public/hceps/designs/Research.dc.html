01 WHY HCI

I started with a BFA in Graphic Design. Early in my career, the work was visual communication — hierarchy, typography, composition, branding. I still think of it as a form of art, but art that has to do something.

Over time, I became increasingly unsatisfied stopping at the mockup. I wanted to understand what happened after handoff — why interfaces behaved the way they did, how accessibility shaped the experience, how technical constraints altered the final product.

That curiosity pulled me into the frontend. HTML and CSS felt like composition; JavaScript introduced behavior; React introduced systems; APIs helped me understand what sat underneath the interface. Over time, design and engineering stopped feeling like opposites.

What is this supposed to help someone do, and how should the entire system support that purpose?

THE QUESTION, EXPANDING HOW SHOULD IT LOOK? HOW SHOULD IT WORK? WHAT DOES IT MAKE SOMEONE CAPABLE OF?

04 DIRECTION
